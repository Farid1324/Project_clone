# Artwork and font provenance

Updated November 23, 2026 (Asia/Baku). Keymarket is an independent internship demo, unaffiliated with Eneba, publishers or storefronts. The catalog now uses downloaded game artwork and clearly marked demo gift-card compositions. Attribution does not grant an open redistribution licence: artwork and trademarks remain the property of their respective rights holders. No licence to resell artwork or claim official affiliation is asserted.

## Current catalog covers

All **45 seeded listings** have appropriate locally served artwork: **36 distinct game covers** for 37 game variants and **eight gift-card designs**. Global and US Stardew Valley variants share one cover. There are **44 optimized 600×900 WebP files** in backend/public/demo/covers, totaling **3,871,910 bytes** versus 8,628,611 downloaded bytes (55.1% smaller). No title remains assigned generic geometry or the unavailable fallback.

The authoritative [asset manifest](../backend/database/seeders/data/cover-artwork.json) records each local path, all covered slugs, source pages and original image URLs, retrieval timestamps, source/output dimensions and hashes, processing and edition checks. Downloads were obtained on November 22 UTC / November 23 locally. React reads the existing product resource's cover_image_url; no cover selection or remote API credentials are embedded in the frontend. No third-party API key is needed at seed time or runtime.

Most games use publisher-supplied [Steam library capsules](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/libraryassets), whose portrait format suits the cards. Xbox and Nintendo storefront art fills edition-specific gaps. Correct title/edition identity was checked against storefront metadata and all 44 final assets were reviewed visually. Regional variants may share the same underlying title artwork; the API's platform, edition and activation-region notices remain authoritative.

Specific selection decisions:

- Halo Infinite Campaign uses Xbox's neutral Master Chief poster. Steam's MULTIPLAYER cover was rejected; the campaign entitlement is separate from free multiplayer.
- Diablo IV uses Xbox Standard Edition Lilith artwork. A seasonal Steam image was rejected.
- Minecraft: Xbox One Edition uses Walmart's legacy Xbox One Edition box front, supported by the original Xbox announcement. Modern Bedrock artwork was rejected. White retailer margins were removed, retaining the full cover branding.
- Nintendo game covers are original Switch US storefront box fronts, not Switch 2 or a different edition. US rating marks on the illustration do not change the listing's stated Europe activation region.
- Forza Horizon 5, Sea of Thieves, Flight Simulator 2020, Gears 5 and Ori use platform-neutral publisher Steam artwork for the same game. Flight Simulator's free 40th Anniversary artwork identifies the 2020 Standard base game, not the 2024 game or Deluxe content.
- Steam's current Sekiro app name includes the free GOTY update; the selected cover does not advertise a paid bonus SKU. The Witcher artwork identifies Wild Hunt without Complete Edition branding. Resident Evil 4 is the 2023 remake. Disco Elysium artwork explicitly identifies The Final Cut.
- Gift cards are **demo compositions**, not scans of issued/redeemable vouchers. Steam and PlayStation use official currency-neutral brand/card art plus the seeded EUR amount. Xbox uses Microsoft's neutral green gift-card image plus EUR amounts; rewards-program expiry terms are not represented as marketplace entitlements. Nintendo €15 Mario and €25 Yoshi use exact-denomination Smyths card art. Compositions preserve full artwork, state the value and include a demo label. No misleading USD amounts or redeemable codes are shown.

## Exact image-generation prompt

Generated through the built-in image-generation tool, opaque background, with no reference image input:

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: a wide landscape promotional background for an independent digital game marketplace internship demo. Create original cinematic fantasy key art: a tiny cloaked traveler on a rocky ridge looking toward an immense ruined citadel and distant mountains beneath a luminous lavender sky, with subtle gold light and drifting mist. Rich deep violet shadows, magenta accents, amber highlights; visually detailed, premium game-poster illustration. Composition: the atmospheric landscape and citadel occupy the right two thirds; the left third stays very dark violet with gentle atmosphere and clean negative space for HTML promotion text. Wide landscape around 2.5:1. No text, no logos, no UI, no real game characters, no franchise symbols, no recognizable copyrighted artwork, no watermark. This is fictional illustrative demo artwork, not an official game cover.

The generated scene was visually inspected before use. HTML overlays the API-managed promotion title, subtitle and navigation; text is not baked into the illustration. The UI labels the illustration and the entire service as demo content. AI-generated originality does not constitute an independent legal clearance opinion.
